This release restores OpenLOCK connections for non-commercial printing.

* Editor, launcher and ordinary application source: MIT, except the explicitly attributed OpenLOCK profile below.
* Original connector-free procedural sculpture: CC0-1.0 (`ASSET-LICENSE.txt`). Previous CC0 grants, including the earlier Foundry Link release, are not revoked.

* OpenLOCK-socketed scenery derivatives: CC BY-NC 4.0. Their raw original sculpture remains available under CC0, but the socketed derivative must not be presented as unrestricted CC0.
* OpenLOCK Clip 5.4 by Printable Scenery: CC BY-NC 4.0. The original file is retained unchanged with its attribution. Export only translates the clip to the print origin; it does not rename it as an original Terrain Foundry invention.

The complete non-commercial licence is supplied at `third-party/openlock/CLIP-LICENSE.txt`, in scenery packs, installer and STL exports. Preserve attribution, identify modifications, provide the licence, and do not use these licensed materials for commercial purposes. MGN Consultancy’s separate Printable Scenery permission is documented below; it does not relicense the community socket code. Other imported models retain their own licences.

CC BY-NC permits sharing and adaptations subject to its terms. Non-commercial assets are not unrestricted open-source assets. The editor remains open-source, with these separately licensed asset components. The licence does not grant trademark rights or imply approval by Printable Scenery. This community socket implementation has not been physically fit-tested; print the supplied floor/wall/clip fit test before a full scene.

Paid print-and-ship checkout remains disabled for this edition unless permissions covering all relevant model and connector rights are separately obtained and reviewed. A licence from one rights holder does not automatically cover another contributor's adaptation.
