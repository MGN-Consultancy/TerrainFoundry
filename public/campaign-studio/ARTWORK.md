Terrain Foundry campaign workshop artwork

The three category illustrations and transparent terrain vignette in this folder were generated as original decorative artwork for the Terrain Foundry desktop campaign workshop. They are interface illustrations, not printable models and do not indicate that any pictured objects are included in an asset pack.

Characters & Figures uses characters-figures-concepts.png, an original generated lineup of a ranger, wizard, tavern keeper, race marshal, train guard and futuristic soldier. Generated 2026-10-12 as decorative concept examples for the interface; these are not library entries or a claim that corresponding printable meshes are included. Railway imagery is reused from the existing site concept artwork.
