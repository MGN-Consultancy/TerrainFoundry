A PLAYBOOK FOR YOUR TABLE

From first idea
to game night.

Keep the details connected

Organise characters, locations, missions, notes, relationships and timelines. Keep DM secrets separate from player handouts. Publish selected player-visible content to an online portal and invite players by email once the hosted service is configured.

Run the session

Open a full-window studio with resizable panes, then run a fullscreen playbook with clickable sound cues beside encounters and missions. Dice, initiative and a soundboard support the session. On-screen controls and keyboard shortcuts provide a route for Stream Deck buttons.

Print the adventure

Prepare campaign cards and a campaign book, with player-safe handouts and duplex printing guidance. Add your own artwork or request generated illustrations through a supported provider.

Optional AI. Your choice of provider.

In Setup, select OpenAI or Claude and add your own API key. AI generation needs internet and uses your provider account; provider charges are separate from Terrain Foundry. Saving and organising your campaign stays local.

Campaign context you send to AI is shared with the provider you choose. Keys must stay outside campaign exports. ChatGPT and Claude subscription access is separate from API access.

The campaign studio source remains open source. Premium scenery collections are separate paid asset packs.
