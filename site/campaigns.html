FROM YOUR FIRST IDEA TO GAME NIGHT

A guide beside you,
not homework.

Start with a short description. The campaign builder can ask friendly follow-up questions, offer sensible choices and help you decide what to work on next.

01 / PLAN

Shape the campaign

Develop the setting, themes, player hooks, campaign length, key characters and an outline for each chapter.

02 / WRITE

Make playable sessions

Turn locations into scenes, missions, choices and consequences. Keep private DM notes separate from player handouts.

03 / BUILD

Connect story to scenery

Ask for encounter layouts using your installed terrain. Review suggestions, then edit the scene in the desktop builder.

04 / ILLUSTRATE

Give it a visual identity

Generate campaign covers and scene art through a supported image model, then save the images with your campaign files.

TALK OR TYPE

Think out loud.
Build as you go.

Use ordinary text chat, dictate a thought, or choose a realtime voice conversation. Explain the idea in your own words; ask for options when you are unsure; request a change when a suggestion does not fit.

  • Voice and dictation are optional tools, not required setup.
  • Choose standard campaign chat or a live voice model in AI settings.
  • Ask the assistant what is already in your campaign, then continue from there.
  • Choose review-first suggestions or enable workspace agent actions in the client settings.

You remain in control. Generated writing and images can be reviewed and edited. The app cannot replace your rules books or decide how your table must play.

OPTIONAL MEANS OPTIONAL

Stay offline, or
bring your own AI.

The map editor, installed scenery and local project files do not require an AI account. If you enable AI, you connect it yourself in the client. Your provider bills AI usage directly according to your account and model.

OpenAI API key settings ↗
01

Create your own API key

Make an OpenAI platform account if needed, add a payment method there and create a secret API key. ChatGPT Plus and an API account are billed separately.

02

Enter it inside the client

Open Terrain Foundry → AI settings → OpenAI. Paste the key directly into the desktop app. Never post it in chat, a campaign note or a public file.

03

Choose models and test

Select Luna, Astra, voice or image generation, then try a small prompt. You can remove the key or turn AI off at any time.

04

Know what leaves your PC

Only the prompt and campaign context needed for your request are sent to the provider you select. The provider processes that request under its own terms. Campaign files stay in your local workspace.
