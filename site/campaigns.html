AI IS OPTIONAL · PERSONAL OPENAI API REQUIRED

Your key. Your choice.

The editor works without AI. Online AI requests use your own provider account and are billed separately.

01

Set up your API account

Create an OpenAI platform account, configure API billing and create a secret key. API usage is separate from a ChatGPT subscription.

OpenAI API settings ↗
02

Connect inside the app

Open AI setup in Terrain Foundry, enter your key and select your chat, voice and image models. Model access depends on your provider account.

03

Choose what to use

Type, dictate or talk live. Requests send your prompt and relevant campaign context to the selected provider; live voice also sends audio. Review generated content before play.

Local projects. Optional online services.

Your saved campaign stays on your PC. Choosing AI involves online processing under the provider’s terms. Turn it off and continue editing, using your runbook and playing local sounds offline.
