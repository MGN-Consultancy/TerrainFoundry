A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

Design here.
Print at home.

Build on a grid, export a print pack, then slice the pieces for your printer. You can also use the editor just to plan encounters and worlds.

01 / CHOOSE

Start with terrain

Browse installed scenery by category, inspect previews, then add pieces to your scene.

02 / ARRANGE

Build the encounter

Place floors, walls, doors, curves and details. Change position or elevation as your design grows.

03 / EXPAND

Make a world

Keep small encounters as reusable projects, then set buildings and scenes into a larger world.

04 / SAVE

Keep your projects local

Save to your computer, reopen later, and export files for printing when you are ready.

CAMPAIGNS, WORLDS AND ENCOUNTERS

One workshop.
Many kinds of worlds.

Build a dungeon room, save it as an encounter, and use it again inside a village or wider campaign world. The campaign studio can help plan the story and suggest layouts that use scenery available in your library.

  • AI chat, dictation, realtime voice and generated artwork are optional.
  • Use your own OpenAI API key for OpenAI models. Provider usage is billed to your account.
  • Your maps and campaign files remain on your PC; AI prompts go to the provider you select.
  • Turn AI off and continue to design, save and export offline.

OFFLINE BY CHOICE

Your computer. Your projects.

After installation, terrain editing, local campaign work, saving and exporting do not need an internet connection. Internet is used only for optional downloads and updates, premium pack activation, AI services or online print quotes.
