GETTING STARTED

Your next game night
starts on your desktop.

Download once. Build at your pace. Keep your files.

01 / INSTALL

A small launcher.

Download the Windows installer. The launcher fetches the editor and scenery, and offers updates when you’re online.

02 / CREATE

Make a place to play.

Use the tutorial, choose a scenery category and begin placing pieces. Save encounters, worlds and campaigns locally.

03 / ENJOY

Play it. Print it.

Run your adventure with notes and sound cues. Export terrain files for your 3D printer, or ask us to print them.

OFFLINE BY CHOICE

It’s your world.
Keep it on your PC.

Once the app and scenery are installed, build terrain, write campaigns, save worlds and encounters, play local sounds and export print files without an internet connection. No required account. No cloud campaign storage.

Go online only when you choose: downloads, updates, premium activation, optional AI or a print-service request.
