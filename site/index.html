THE CAMPAIGN WORKSHOP

OPTIONAL CAMPAIGN COMPANION

Your story.
A creative partner.

Plan a campaign from its first idea to game night. The companion can help shape the setting, write scenarios, develop characters and missions, map player choices and consequences, and build a playbook beside your world.

  • Start with questions and suggestions made for a first-time campaign writer.
  • Ask for original cover and scene illustrations that match your campaign’s visual style.
  • Describe a place in chat or by voice, then ask the builder to suggest terrain from your installed library.
  • Choose review mode or let Agent mode apply supported edits to your campaign workspace, with activity history and undo.

Optional AI (requires your own OpenAI API key for OpenAI models). AI features need internet and are billed by your provider. You can leave them off and keep using the editor offline.
