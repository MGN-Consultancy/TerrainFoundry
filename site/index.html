THE WORKSHOP

Small pieces.
Endless adventures.

Lay out a ruined keep, a winding cavern or a wildflower clearing. Work at tabletop scale with a library of original scenery.

01 / BUILD

Shape your encounter

Place floors, walls, doors, trees and props. Snap pieces together, move them by dragging, and adjust their elevation. Wide curves and 45° wall sections give your rooms space to breathe.

02 / KEEP

Your scenes stay yours

Save editable projects to your computer. Open them offline and keep building. Recovery files help protect work in progress. Updates do not replace your personal projects.

03 / GROW

A library that can grow

The small launcher downloads the editor and scenery from GitHub. It checks for signed updates when online and reuses unchanged packs. The installed editor works without an internet connection.

BEYOND THE DUNGEON

Let the outside in.

Meadows, flowers, shrubs and trees. Rivers and bridges. Weathered cave walls and broad stone curves. Original scenery, with sculpted detail that becomes part of the print.

Preview colours and fine material shading are not included in STL files.

FROM SCENE TO TABLE

Design here.
Slice in Bambu Studio.

Export an STL print pack with separate terrain pieces, a quantity list and your editable project. Open the STLs in Bambu Studio, choose your printer and material, then inspect supports and sliced layers.

The OpenLOCK edition uses an attributed OpenLOCK socket profile and clip under CC BY-NC 4.0 for non-commercial printing. Print the supplied fit test first; physical fit is not yet verified. Keep attribution and licence notices with shared files.

  1. 1Build and save a scene locally
  2. 2Export your terrain and connectors
  3. 3Set up and slice in Bambu Studio
  4. 4Print, assemble and play

NO PRINTER? NO PROBLEM.

Print at home.
For your own table.

OpenLOCK parts in this edition are for non-commercial use. Our paid printing service is paused unless the necessary commercial permissions are obtained. You can still export your scenes and print them yourself under the supplied licences.

  1. In the editor, choose Prepare print pack.
  2. Compress the exported folder into a ZIP. Keep the STL files and quantities.csv together.
  3. Choose one PLA colour and enter your delivery details.
  4. Review your emailed quote, then pay securely with PayPal when you are ready.

No account needed. This optional service uploads manufacturing files to us; your normal editor projects still stay on your computer. We check every paid order in Bambu Studio before printing.
