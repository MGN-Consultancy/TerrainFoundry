From purchase to your terrain library

  1. Choose a pack. Browse its complete piece catalogue before buying.
  2. Receive your code by email. Your purchase email includes the pack code and setup instructions.
  3. Redeem in the Windows client. Enter the code to activate your device and download the pack.
  4. Build offline. Once installed, use the pack without a continuous internet connection.

Pack licences count activated devices. Download retries and updates on an activated device do not use another activation. The free Deepstone starter remains free.
