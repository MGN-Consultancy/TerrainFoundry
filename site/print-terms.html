What you order

We supply your uploaded terrain as unpainted, single-colour PLA prints. The chosen filament colour applies to the whole order. Screen colours are an illustration; physical filament can vary. Only listed STL models and quantities are included. Connectors and fit-test pieces in your exported quantity list are included in the price. We inspect slicing in Bambu Studio before printing; geometric checks do not guarantee printability or connector fit.

Quotes and payment

Quotes use enclosed model volume, quantity, setup, shipping and applicable VAT under the tariff shown with your quote. Model volume is not a prediction of filament consumption, infill, supports or printing time. Quotes are valid for seven days. Payment is taken through PayPal only after you choose to proceed. Payment must be confirmed by PayPal before an order is sent to the workshop. If we cannot manufacture your order as specified, we will contact you to agree a change or refund.

Your rights and your files

Upload only models you have permission to have printed. You keep your rights in your files and give MGN CONSULTANCY LIMITED permission to process them to quote, print and fulfil this order. This permission does not allow publishing your models or using them for unrelated projects. Nothing here removes your statutory consumer rights, including rights concerning faulty or misdescribed goods.

Optional upload privacy

The Terrain Foundry editor continues to save scenes locally without an account. This optional print service is different: submitting a request uploads your print pack, email, colour choice and delivery address to private Azure storage so we can quote and fulfil your order. We keep the manufacturing files; editable project files and unrelated documents are discarded during processing. PayPal processes payment and delivery information; Azure Communication Services delivers order emails. We do not receive your card details.

We retain unpaid requests for up to 30 days and paid order records and files for up to 180 days in this service. Transaction records in PayPal and any business accounting records may have separate legally required retention periods. Paid-order download links sent to the workshop expire after 24 hours. The private quote link should not be shared publicly. Cloudflare Turnstile protects the form against automated abuse.

Contact nigel.webster@mgnconsultancy.co.uk for order queries, cancellation requests or requests to access or delete your personal information. We may need to retain information required for accounting or legal obligations.
