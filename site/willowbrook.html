Reinstalling or replacing your computer?

You do not need to buy the pack again. Keep your purchase email and reuse your code. Download retries and pack updates on the same activation do not consume another licence slot.

If a Windows reinstall or replacement computer needs an activation reset, email nigel.webster@mgnconsultancy.co.uk from your purchase email address. Include your order reference, pack name and whether the old computer is still available. We will check your purchase and help release the old activation at no extra charge. Never email passwords or payment-card details.
